MP-KG-RAG research archive

Four variants with 1,550 final rows each and 7,750 perspective records. The archive supports reproducibility and failure analysis; it does not establish uniformly better semantic answer quality.

TerraServe fixed-set evaluation

Archived MobileNetV3-Small predictions contain 202 correct classifications out of 210 supplied evaluation tiles. Geographic independence and generalization remain unestablished.

ForgeSight calibration failure

An assumed linear peak-RSS fit produced an invalid negative intercept. Calibration reports the fit as invalid and falls back to the largest measured peak.
